# Investor / stakeholder meeting pack

## Suggested 25-minute agenda

1. **4min — Problem:** scheduling, trustworthy teachers, forgetting memorization, lack of evidence of progress, difficult teacher continuity. Existing competitors validated; no overstated TAM.
2. **5min — Product:** unified learner account, qualified live teacher, approved learning map, adaptive review, exams, competitions; no unverified Quranic AI verdicts.
3. **4min — Tech:** provider-agnostic meeting with external Meet/Zoom limitations, Laravel API-first and Flutter roadmap, privacy + contest judging.
4. **5min — Business:** monthly subscriptions, per-teacher unit cost, variable compensation salary/hour/lesson/shift/hybrid, localized cohorts and CAC/renewal; scenarios explicitly hypothetical.
5. **4min — Milestones:** P0 paid pilot, P1 learning/contests, AI evaluated pilot, P2 mobile; capital tranches based on gates.
6. **3min — Ask:** confirm funding envelope, owners, entity/market, risk acceptance, academic director and consent approvals.

## Investor questions and evidence status

| Question | Answer prepared | Data status |
|---|---|---|
| Why not just Google Meet? | Fine for external lessons; custom features/audio not equivalent; provider policy | Documented constraints, not tested POC |
| Why won't teachers poach customers? | Service value, contacts policy, legal agreements, supervised flag review; no perfect guarantee | Hypothesis needs operating evidence |
| Why stay vs Tarteel? | Humans + academy support + approved learning journey; Tarteel already has AI error detection | Product hypothesis |
| How many learners to break even? | Depends price mix, full-loaded teaching, churn, staff and media costs | Scenario file only |
| How will competition drive retention? | Match levels and test revision improvement/renewal | Unmeasured until cohort A/B |
| Can AI certify correct tajweed? | No; human judge, specialist model evaluated later | Safety restriction |
| Is product investment ready? | Ready for discovery/controlled build; not yet commercial proof | Needs pilot |

## Approvals to ask investors

- Funding tranche and burn cap; explicit equity/IP agreement.
- Product authority to ship adult education immediately once legal, teacher, payment and safeguarding gates pass; minor enrollment only with legal consent.
- Approval for academic supervisor and 3–5 vetted teachers in pilot.
- Budget for POC of 2 video vendors + AI evaluations before purchasing annual contracts.
- Acceptance that **full production feature set is a roadmap** and should not delay first revenue until all advanced AI passes.
