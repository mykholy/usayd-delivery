# Go-to-Market and 180-day marketing plan

## Positioning

**Core promise:** Learn the Quran with a qualified teacher, a plan suited to your level, verified revision and visible progress between lessons. We are **not** claiming a first-ever Quran AI or guaranteed outcomes.

## Acquisition channel tests

1. **Organic proof:** 10 short clips with actual consenting qualified teachers; topics include common reading mistakes, how to review after a break, how a lesson assessment works, and teacher's weekly plan. Never film children for marketing without additional valid permission.
2. **Meta:** adult-directed ad campaigns by market/landing page; never target people based on inferred religious beliefs where policy prohibits; approved scripts only.
3. **Google Search:** high-intent keyword groups around online Quran lessons / tajweed / memorization, country-specific pages and negative keywords; review platform sensitive-targeting policies.
4. **Referral:** credit or authorized free lessons after successful first paid renewal, with anti-fraud limits; no contest vote buying.
5. **Content/SEO:** useful human-reviewed Arabic/English curriculum explanations, not mass AI pages; reviewer byline and academic accuracy check.
6. **Partnerships:** educators/family communities in Egypt and Gulf; signed disclosures for sponsored referrals.

## Experimental funnel

`Qualified lead → Assessment booked → Assessment attended → Paid start → First lesson → First renewal → D90 retention`.

Track segment, date, ad source, attribution, landing page, consent, teacher, package, payment attempt and renewal cohort. Reconcile conversion against unique, eligible people; do not use form submissions as paid student counts.

## Initial 90 days

| Days | Activities | Evidence gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1–14 | Recruit 3–5 teachers; interview 30–50 target adults/parents; create 2 landing pages; finish legal consent and payment test | 10 strongly qualified sales opportunities |
| 15–30 | Manual/automated trial scheduling, launch minimal paid courses, track every complaint | 10–20 paid starts and documented teacher satisfaction |
| 31–60 | 3 creative hypotheses × 2 segments, UTM tracking, test 2 prices, improve lesson quality | First renewal and cohort CAC/contribution |
| 61–90 | Scale only qualified winning cohort, publish approved progress stories, referral trial | Stable schedule and teacher capacity |
| 91–180 | Add level-appropriate competitions, adaptive review demos, add one region if economics permit | D60 retention, CAC payback, session quality |

## Sample 3 ad concepts (drafts for creative review)

- **Teacher + plan:** "مش مجرد مكالمة: حد يحدد مستواك ويتابع حفظك ومراجعتك خطوة بخطوة." CTA: تقييم مستوى.
- **Return after interruption:** "وقفت حفظ فترة؟ نبدأ بمراجعة مناسبة لمستواك ونبني خطة رجوع." CTA: اختر مواعيدك.
- **Competition after shipping:** "اختبر حفظك في تحديات تعليمية حسب مستواك، وخد تقريرًا يفيدك حتى لو مش ضمن الفائزين." Never market competition before shipping it.

## Campaign dashboard

Weekly: spend, qualified leads, trial show-up, cost/trial, cost/first paid, renewal, paid cohort contribution, instructor quality, refunds, safety incidents. Stop ads if payment failure, unstaffed schedule or quality/safeguarding thresholds are breached.

## Competition growth guardrails

Competitions do not require extra paid attempts, ranking minors is private or opt-in pseudonymized by lawful permission, no exploitative urgency/shame, no pay-to-win prizes. Achievements improve learning, not push compulsory daily usage or sleep-disrupting reminders.

## Marketing readiness checklist

Public policy pages; pricing & cancellation accurate; payment tested; first teachers profiled and vetted; demo real; no false accreditation or fabricated testimonials; analytics consent; creative approved; customer support able to respond; valid country payment method.
