# Teacher hiring, scheduling, payment and academic operations SOP

## Teacher lifecycle

Application → identity/qualification check → Quran/tajweed evaluation → child safeguarding interview (where applicable) → demo lesson → references → contract & privacy training → probation → active scheduling → QA → periodic reassessment → offboarding.

### Minimum operating procedures

- Verify certificate/ijazah claims before public display; no unverified `Al-Azhar accredited` labels.
- Written expectations for professionalism, lesson delivery, teacher substitution, record completion and guardian communication.
- All learner contacts routed through academy support or permitted platform messaging; no personal solicitation. Respect lawful direct contact rights; cannot guarantee preventing spoken leakage.
- Student safeguarding training, reporting, supervision and immediate escalation of threats. Separate safety decisions from AI flags.
- Pre-lesson check: device/network/Quran lesson context; post-lesson rubric and task assignment within agreed time.
- Capacity planning includes prep, breaks, weekly off, timezone availability and buffers. Calendar conflicts are blocked transactionally.

## Teacher compensation mechanisms

| Model | Formula sketch | Good for | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed salary | monthly agreed gross + legally required employer obligations | predictable full-time load | idle time, labor compliance |
| Per lesson | sum(eligible lesson fee per class) | initial variable demand | unpredictability, lesson gaming |
| Per taught hour | verified eligible minutes / 60 × hourly rate | variable duration classes | inflated meeting time |
| Per reserved shift | shifts × payable rate minus authorized adjustments | planned timetables | empty time risk |
| Hybrid | min guarantee + variable over threshold + capped bonuses | retention and incentives | complexity/contract disputes |
| Small group | group teaching base + incremental per seat up to cap | matched cohorts | teacher incentive not to overstuff |

All rules **versioned** by effective date and jurisdiction with minimum wage/legal checks. Payout never calculated from a mutable historical contract. Finance reviews every manual adjustment and no-show classification. Teacher gets statement and appeal channel.

## Attendance-driven eligibility matrix

| Case | Student credit | Teacher compensation | Approver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completed valid lesson | consumed | according to contract | automatic + audit |
| Student absent | based on cancellation/no-show policy | show-up or reserved slot as contracted | policy engine |
| Teacher absent | credit returned or free makeup | no lesson fee, possible contractual handling | supervisor |
| Provider outage | reschedule/credit | contract-specific fair treatment | ops + finance |
| Lesson interrupted | partial or rebook based on verified duration/quality | prorate/approved adjustment | ops |
| Contest judging | no lesson credit | judging contract compensation | supervisor |

## Quality program

Monthly classroom checklist: appropriate lesson objective, correct Quran source and recitation, patience, learner feedback, report quality, on-time rate, and teacher safeguarding compliance. Complaints reviewed by human with documented evidence and appeal.

## Staffing for pilot

3–5 verified teachers with backup coverage, 1 qualified academic lead, owner-operated sales/support at start, part-time finance/legal support, CTO+developer(s). Do not overhire salaried shifts before stable demand or treat volunteer work as free at scale.
