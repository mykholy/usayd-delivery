# BRD — Business Requirements Document
**Doc ID:** BRD-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Owner:** Business/Product | **Approval:** Sponsor + Academic Lead + Finance

## Investment thesis
A managed, subscription-based online Quran academy for learners of any age, combining qualified live instruction, assessed progress, adaptive revision, responsible AI assistants and fair competitions. The institution, not a teacher's personal phone number or meeting link, provides the reliable learning journey.

## Problem statement
Learners struggle with choosing a trustworthy teacher, coordinating schedules, preserving continuity after teacher changes, systematic revision, monitoring progress and returning after lapses. Existing services already address several needs; the differentiation hypothesis is **end-to-end connected service quality** rather than an unsupported claim of being the first AI/Quran product.

## Target and segmentation

| Segment | Initial pain | Offer | Willingness to pay hypothesis | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Egyptian adult beginner | Needs confidence, time flexibility | Assessment + 1:1 plan | Price-sensitive | Paid landing-page experiment |

## Business objectives and metrics

1. **Demand:** 10–20 paid pilot learners across at least two segments. Do not count leads or trials as customers.
2. **Instructional quality:** measurable on-time lesson rate, rubric completion and qualified teacher consistency; monthly academic review.
3. **Retention:** first renewal per cohort, later D30/D60/D90 paid retention. Targets are *gates*, not industry benchmarks.
4. **Economics:** positive contribution after teacher time, video, AI, fees and support; monthly CAC payback review.
5. **Safeguarding:** no unresolved high-severity incidents; well-documented reporting and escalation for minors.

## Revenue streams

- `R1` Core individual subscription: 8, 12, 16 lessons per 4-week/monthly billing cycle (billing calendar to be confirmed contractually).
- `R2` Level-matched small groups, approved only after safeguarding, quality and economics checks.
- `R3` Recorded course access or bundle where content rights are held.
- `R4` AI Practice/Study Plus optional tier after benchmark and cost cap; basic assigned practice stays in core.
- `R5` Future B2B organizations and white-label only upon validated demand and authorization boundaries.
- Avoid per-quiz purchase that affects contest odds; awards are not gambling or lotteries.

## Product value proposition

- Learner journey belongs to academy: assessment → curriculum → live sessions → teacher-approved report → daily practice → adaptive revision → evaluation → competitions → renewal.
- Teachers are accredited by platform standards; plans and progress are portable between teachers.
- Accessible RTL/Arabic-first UX; audio-only and network recovery.
- Operational transparency: attendance, make-ups, invoices, teacher performance and appeals.

## Competitor evidence and gaps

Public competitor offers demonstrate price competition. Tarteel already offers AI mistake detection, so we must not market it as unique. Online academies already sell lesson plans and reports. Market-size totals based on total Muslim population or internet subscribers are not credible evidence of a serviceable paying audience. See `08_REFERENCES/SOURCES_AND_VALIDATION.md` for evidence and `01_BUSINESS/UNIT_ECONOMICS.md` for testable assumptions.

## Commercial policy decisions required

- Prices are configured by country/currency, tax rules, lesson duration, renewal, coupon and teacher cost model.
- Define refund/no-show/teacher absence/tech outage/credit expiry in customer-readable Terms, signed off before checkout.
- Renewal is manual until a legally permitted, user-consented recurring scheme is integrated.
- Teacher compensation must account for local labor laws and employer contributions; no assumption that cash stipend equals fully-loaded cost.

## In-scope / out-of-scope boundaries

**In-scope P0:** Learner identity, minor consent gate, teacher approval, programs, schedule, 1:1 live, real paid subscriptions, reports, support, attendance, safeguarding, security.

**In-scope P1:** Contests, adaptive revision, approved assessment bank, teacher-approved AI plans, transcript processing under legal permissions, rewards, recorded lessons, mobile-optimized web.

**In-scope P2+:** Independent verified recitation AI, Flutter Android/iOS apps, open multi-tenant school product, live automatic intervention, broad languages, institutional franchise.

Not promised: official educational accreditation, guaranteed memorization outcome, AI issuing fatwas, perfectly preventing outside contact, feature parity across conferencing providers.

## Dependencies

- Qualified academic director and rubric/competitions reviewers.
- Local legal counsel: e-commerce/education/religious representations, minors, data and cross-border payment.
- Video vendor feasibility and cost; cancellation/reconciliation integration.
- Teacher compensation contract and capacity planning.
- Content licenses and protected Quran text pipeline.

## Funding hypothesis and release decisions

Recommended staged underwriting: small paid pilot → proven course completion and first renewals → evidence-based customer acquisition → scalable workflows → advanced AI/competitions. Investment committee should request real cohort metrics rather than rely on scenario tables alone.
